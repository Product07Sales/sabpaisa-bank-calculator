Diagnosis

FY 25-26: Seven Core Issues & Our Strategic Response

1. Cross-Sell Near Zero (93% use 1 product)

Response: Product bundling (PG+Forms/Links). AM KPI: ≥30% clients use 2+ products.

2. Single Product Dependency (PG = 99.76% txns)

Response: 5-pod diversification. NACH, BBPS, xChain, PA-P each with independent revenue targets.

3. ~60% Inactive Base (Dead + Non-Transacting)

Response: Activation campaigns (first txn within 7 days). Reactivate dead clients. Lifecycle engagement.

4. PLG Almost Zero (141 self-sourced out of 2,860)

Response: QwikPaisa self-serve LIVE. PLG pull ratio 10% → 40-50% by Q4. T2F <10 min.

5. Channel Imbalance (Direct 47% clients but 25% GMV)

Response: Scale Bank channel (34% clients = 44% GMV). Channel-specific pricing. Optimize Referral monetization.

6. Bank Concentration (BOI 46% of bank clients)

Response: Activate dormant banks (BoB 4, BoM 2, CBI 11). Dedicated bank GTM plans. Double down on top 3.

7. Ticket Size Crash (₹4,140 → ₹815)

Response: Push subscription billing. Focus higher-ticket segments (Education, B2B). Minimum MDR/platform fee.

Every issue has a corresponding action in this AOP. This is not a growth plan built on hope — it is a diagnostic response to structural problems.
